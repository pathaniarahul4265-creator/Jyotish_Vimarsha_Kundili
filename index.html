What if the sky

For thousands of years, people have watched the movement of the Sun, Moon and planets and searched for meaning in the rhythm of time. Astrology begins with that human curiosity: observing the heavens and using celestial cycles as a symbolic language for understanding life, temperament, relationships, choices and changing seasons.

Jyotish, the Indian tradition of astrology, takes that idea further through a birth chart — a symbolic map calculated for a particular moment and place. The chart brings together Rashis (zodiac signs), Bhavas (houses), Grahas (planetary influences), Nakshatras (lunar mansions), Yogas and Dashas to explore both what a pattern may signify and when it may become more prominent.

SIDEREAL MECHANICS & CLASSICAL PARASHARI CONCEPTS

Celestial Architecture at a Glance

Essential astronomical and Vedic astrological principles that form the foundation of your Kundli and planetary readings.

ASTRONOMY & AYANAMSHA 01

Sidereal Zodiac & Lahiri Precession

Vedic Astrology uses the Sidereal Zodiac, fixed to physical stellar constellations. Earth's axis precesses ~1° every 72 years (~25,772-year Great Year). The difference between Tropical 0° Aries and Sidereal 0° Aries is the Ayanamsha (currently 24°10'38" Lahiri), ensuring planetary longitudes match exact astronomical positions.

LUNAR MANSIONS 02

27 Nakshatras & Vimshottari Dasha

The 360° zodiac is divided into 27 lunar mansions (Nakshatras) of 13°20' each. The Moon visits one Nakshatra daily. Your birth Moon's Nakshatra degree sets your Janma Nakshatra and unlocks your 120-year Vimshottari Dasha sequence — charting major timing windows of life.

DIVISIONAL CHARTS 03

Navamsha (D9) — The Soul's Mirror

While the birth chart (D1) represents physical manifestation, each sign is subdivided into 9 harmonic arcs of 3°20' to form the Navamsha (D9) chart. It reveals planetary micro-dignity (Vargottama), marital harmony, and inner spiritual potential maturing after age 30.

HOUSES OF LIFE 04

12 Bhavas: Kendras & Trikonas

The 12 houses span life from vitality (1st / Lagna) to career (10th). The Kendra houses (1, 4, 7, 10) form the structural pillars of destiny, while the Trikona houses (1, 5, 9) signify Lakshmi's divine grace, past-life merit (Purva Punya), and wisdom.

PLANETARY MOTIONS 05

Retrograde (Vakra) & Cheshta Bala

When Earth overtakes an outer planet, the planet appears to move backward in Retrograde (Vakra) motion. In Parashari astrology, retrograde planets gain high Cheshta Bala (effort strength), indicating deep karmic re-evaluation, intensity, and unconventional talent.

COMPATIBILITY 06

Ashtakoot Guna Milan (36 Points)

Vedic relationship matching evaluates 8 dimensions of energetic harmony between lunar birth positions. Key metrics include Graha Maitri (mental friendship - 5 pts), Yoni (instinctive compatibility - 4 pts), Bhakoot (emotional wavelength - 7 pts), and Nadi (genetic & physiological balance - 8 pts).
